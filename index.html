About Us

Two seasons, one crew, zero athletes left behind.

The Pirates Cross Country and Track & Field Booster Club is an independent, volunteer-led nonprofit made up of parents, guardians, alumni and community members who care about our distance and track programs.

School budgets cover the basics. They do not cover a second set of racing uniforms, entry fees for the invitational everyone wants to run, the tent and tables at the far end of a hillside course, the post-race fruit, the timing support, or the banquet at the end of a long season. That is our job.

We fundraise, we organize, we show up early with the canopy and stay late to fold it back up — so the coaches can coach and the athletes can race.

Our mission: raise the funds and the hands that let every Pirate athlete train, travel and compete without a financial barrier.

  • Independent of the school. We partner with the coaching staff, but we are governed by our own elected parent board.
  • One class of membership. Every member has an equal voice and an equal vote.
  • Every dollar stays with the athletes. No paid staff, no salaries, no exceptions.

What We Fund

The unglamorous things that make a season work

01

Uniforms & Spikes

Racing singlets, warmups and a spike-assistance fund so no athlete lines up in the wrong shoes because of cost.

02

Meet Operations

Entry fees, course markers, timing help, canopies, water and the volunteer crew that runs the team tent at every meet.

03

Team Meals & Fuel

Pre-race hydration, post-race recovery snacks and hydration at practice through the hottest weeks of August.

04

Travel & Camp

Buses to championship meets, lodging support for postseason travel and scholarships for summer distance camp.

05

Awards & Banquet

End-of-season banquet, letter awards, record-board updates and the senior night every family remembers.

06

Senior Scholarships

Annual scholarships for graduating Pirates who gave the program their four years — funded entirely by member support.

Get Involved

Four steps from spectator to crew

Come to a Meeting

General membership meetings are open to everyone. Bring questions, opinions and a folding chair.

Sign Up

Pay annual dues or commit to two volunteer shifts. Either one makes you a full voting member.

Pick a Job

Tent crew, course marshal, snack table, photography, spirit wear, sponsorship outreach — there is a fit for everyone.

Show Up Saturday

Meets start early and end loud. Bring a hat, cheer for every jersey, and you will be hooked by October.
