Meet Day

First time at a cross country meet?

What to Bring

  • Folding chair and a hat — there is rarely shade
  • Cash or card for parking and entry at invitationals
  • Water for you, not just for your runner
  • Comfortable shoes, you will walk the course

How It Works

  • Races go in waves: frosh/soph, JV, then varsity
  • Team score is the sum of your top five finishers
  • Low score wins — like golf
  • Most XC races are 3 miles or 5,000 meters

Where to Watch

  • Start line, then cut to the mile-two hill
  • Finish chute fills up early — claim a spot
  • Team tent is our meeting point all day
  • Never step into the running lane, ever

Volunteers Needed

  • Tent setup crew, 90 minutes before gun time
  • Course marshals at every home meet
  • Snack and hydration table
  • Photos for the team feed
